## English (en-US / en-CA)

**App name:** CaMoim - Koreans in Canada

**Subtitle (App Store) / Short description (Google Play):**
- App Store subtitle: Korean community in Canada
- Google Play short description: Community for Koreans in Canada — boards, market, jobs, rentals, map and chat

**Promotional text (App Store):**
Where Koreans across Canada meet. Buy and sell, find jobs and rooms, discover Korean businesses on the map and join your school's community — all in one app.

**Keywords (App Store):**
korean,canada,toronto,vancouver,community,international student,working holiday,immigration,jobs

**Description:**

CaMoim is the community app for Koreans living in Canada.

International students, immigrants and working holiday makers from Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary and across Canada come together to share information, talk through everyday worries, and trade the things and jobs they need. Most posts are written in Korean.

[ Features ]

Boards for everyday life
- Free talk and an anonymous board for things that are hard to say out loud
- Meetups: find people to go places and do things with
- Marketplace, cars and giveaways: buy, sell and give away locally
- Jobs and part-time work
- Room rentals, homestays and real estate
- Immigration and PR, study abroad, and working holiday tips
- Currency exchange and scam alerts

Browse by city
See posts for Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary, Edmonton, Ottawa, Winnipeg, Victoria, Halifax and more. Marketplace posts show at a glance whether an item is still available.

Korean business map
Find Korean restaurants, grocery stores, clinics, salons and more on a map, with reviews and ratings, sorted by distance from you.

Find a place to stay
Compare room rentals and homestays on the map by location, price and move-in date.

School communities
Private boards and a school-wide chat lounge for Canadian universities and colleges such as UBC, UofT, McGill, SFU and Waterloo. Only students and alumni verified with a student ID can join.

Groups
Create or join groups for hobbies, study, your city, careers or working holidays. Every group has its own board and group chat.

Real-time chat
Arrange trades and meetups safely in 1:1 chat. Report or block anyone you don't want to hear from.

Notifications
Never miss a comment or like on your post, a chat message or an announcement.

Exchange rate widget
Check today's Canadian dollar to Korean won rate right on the home screen.

A safe community
- Email verification
- Optional school verification
- Report and block
- Moderated by our team

[ Made for ]
- Students preparing to study in Canada or who just arrived
- Working holiday makers
- Families settling in or working toward PR
- Anyone looking for Korean friends at their school or in their city
- Anyone buying, selling or looking for a roommate
